HVX Entertainment Law Support

Access trusted legal resources through our network of independent entertainment attorneys and industry professionals — so every artist has professional guidance to protect their music, business, and career.

  • Contract Review Coordination
  • Recording Agreement Review
  • Distribution and Licensing Agreements
  • Publishing and Copyright Guidance
  • Trademark and Brand Protection Referrals
  • Business Formation Assistance (LLC, Corporation, DBA)
  • Immigration and Visa Attorney Referrals for International Artists
  • Negotiation Support and Legal Consultation Scheduling
  • Intellectual Property Protection Guidance

How It Works: HVX Music acts as a liaison between the artist and independent licensed attorneys. We coordinate communication, facilitate consultations, and help artists access the legal resources they need.

HVX Music does not provide legal advice or legal representation. All legal services are provided exclusively by independent licensed attorneys and legal professionals.

Pricing is customized per matter — request a custom quote.
